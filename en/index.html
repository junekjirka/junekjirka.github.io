Selling to EU buyers? Your listing has to say who made it.

Since 13 December 2024, Article 19 of the EU General Product Safety Regulation requires every online listing to show the manufacturer's name, postal and electronic address, a product identifier and any safety warnings. If the maker is outside the EU, the listing also needs an EU responsible person. The free Listing Check starts with a 2-minute test of whether this applies to you.
